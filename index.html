🔗 Link / Text ₹ UPI Pay 📶 Wi-Fi 💬 WhatsApp 👤 Contact (vCard) ✉ Email 📍 Location (Geo)

⚡ Built-In Offline Engine

Powered by a custom Reed-Solomon QRCore engine supporting Version 1–14 and M, Q, and High error correction—no external servers or internet required.

₹ Instant UPI & Smart Payloads

Generate ready-to-scan UPI payment QR codes with Payee Name, Amount (₹), and Note, plus one-tap Wi-Fi login, WhatsApp chat links, and vCards.

🎨 Custom Colors & Gradients

Personalize every QR code with dual-color linear gradients, solid presets, custom background colors, or a clean transparent background mode.

❖ Body Patterns & Corner Eyes

Switch between Rounded, Dots, or Square modules and match them with Soft Square, Circular, Leaf, or Sharp corner eye styles.

🖼️ Center Logo Embedding

Add your own brand icon or image (up to 5 MB) right in the center with automatic High (H) error correction so codes remain reliably scannable.

📐 Print-Ready PNG Export

Save crisp PNG files in 600px, 1024px (HD), or 2048px (Print) resolution directly to your device, copy raw QR payloads, or share immediately.
